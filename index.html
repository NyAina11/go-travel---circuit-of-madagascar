About Go Travel

Created in October 2008 and operational since January 2009, Go Travel holds its travel agency and tour operator licence since May 2009. It is a travel agency, a destination management company, a car rental company and the Madagascar agent of Europ Assistance, run by an experienced Malagasy team who know the country and its customs. Its aim: offer different ways of discovering Madagascar, with cultural awareness and biodiversity preservation through responsible tourism.
